    Formula Sheet (Lec 6)

    1. Analysis of Singly Reinforced Beam

    Equilibrium: $$ T = C \Rightarrow A_s f_y = 0.85 f_c' a b $$

    $$ a = \frac{A_s f_y}{0.85 f_c' b} \quad , \quad x = \frac{a}{\beta_1} $$

    $$ \epsilon_s = \left( \frac{d - x}{x} \right) 0.003 $$

    $$ M_n = A_s f_y \left( d - \frac{a}{2} \right) $$

    2. Analysis of Doubly Reinforced Beam

    Equilibrium: $$T = C_c + C_s$$

    A. Assuming Compression Steel Yields ($f_s' = f_y$):

    $$a = \frac{A_s f_y - A_s' (f_y - 0.85 f_c')}{0.85 f_c' b}$$

    $$\epsilon_s' = \left( \frac{x - d'}{x} \right) 0.003$$

    B. If Compression Steel NOT Yielded ($\epsilon_s' < \epsilon_y$):

    $$0.85 f_c' (\beta_1 x) b + A_s' \left( \left( \frac{x - d'}{x} \right) 0.003 E_s - 0.85 f_c' \right) = A_s f_y$$

    $$M_n = 0.85 f_c' a b \left( d - \frac{a}{2} \right) + A_s' (f_s' - 0.85 f_c') (d - d')$$

    3. Design of Singly Reinforced Beam

    $$m = \frac{f_y}{0.85 f_c'} \quad , \quad R_n = \rho f_y \left(1 - \frac{1}{2} \rho m \right)$$

    $$b d^2 = \frac{M_u}{\phi R_n} ; \text{assume } \phi = 0.9$$
